FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common electrical questions.

Do I need a permit for electrical work in my home?

Most electrical work requiring changes to your panel, new circuits, or service upgrades does require a permit. Our team handles all permit applications on your behalf, ensuring full compliance with local codes.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

Our emergency team is available 24/7. In most metro areas, we arrive within 45–90 minutes. For immediate threats like sparks, burning smell, or no power, call our emergency line directly at +1 (800) 555-WIRE.

Are your electricians licensed and insured?

Yes. Every WireWise electrician is NEC-certified, fully licensed by the state, and carries comprehensive liability insurance. All technicians are background-checked and uniformed for your peace of mind.

How much does a panel upgrade cost?

Panel upgrade costs vary based on current amperage, new panel size, and local code requirements. Typical residential upgrades range from $1,200–$3,500 including labor and permits. We provide a free written estimate after assessment.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

All WireWise installations come with a 2-year workmanship warranty. If any issue arises from our work within that period, we return and fix it at no charge. Materials also carry manufacturer warranties.

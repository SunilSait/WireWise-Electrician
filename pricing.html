FAQs

Pricing Questions Answered

Are parts and materials included in the plan price?

No — plan prices cover labor only. Materials are billed separately at cost with no markup. We source quality materials and provide a full itemized receipt for all parts used.

Can I get a custom quote for a larger project?

Absolutely. For full home rewiring, new construction, or complex installations, we provide detailed project-specific quotes after a free site assessment. Contact us and we'll schedule a visit.

Do you charge extra for evenings or weekends?

Standard appointments on evenings and weekends are at no extra charge. Emergency after-hours callouts (outside 8am–8pm) carry a $75 emergency response surcharge, which covers dispatch costs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, bank transfer, and cash. Payment is due upon job completion, after you've inspected and approved the work. We never ask for full payment upfront.

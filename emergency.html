Know the Signs

When to Call for Emergency Service

These situations require immediate professional attention. Do not delay.

Sparks or Arcing

Visible sparks from outlets, switches, or your panel are an immediate fire risk. Cut power and call us instantly.

Burning Smell

A burning or plastic-like smell from walls, outlets, or your panel indicates overheating wiring — a fire hazard.

Power Outage

If your circuit breakers aren't tripped but power is out, the issue may be at the service entrance — call us now.

Flickering Lights

Persistent flickering — especially across multiple rooms — signals loose connections or an overloaded circuit.

Frequent Tripping

If a breaker trips repeatedly, it's overloaded or faulty. Resetting it without investigation is dangerous.

Hot Outlets

Warm or discolored outlets/switches indicate faulty wiring beneath the surface — potentially dangerous.

Stay Safe

What to Do Before We Arrive

Follow these steps to minimize risk while our emergency crew is on the way.

1

Stay Calm

Don't panic. Identify where the issue seems to originate and note any visible signs (sparks).

2

Cut Power if Safe

If it is safe to do so, switch off the main breaker at your electrical panel to stop current flow to area.

3

Evacuate if Need

If you smell smoke, see flames, or suspect a fire — evacuate immediately and call the number 911.

4

Call WireWise

Call +1 (800) 555-WIRE. Our dispatcher will guide you through next steps while the team the way.
